# User Stories — BizFlow 360

## 1. Executive & General Management Stories
- **US-MGR-01:** *As a Retail Operations Manager,* I want an executive dashboard displaying total revenue, gross profit, and margin percentages over selected time horizons so that I can evaluate commercial performance without waiting for month-end accounting.
- **US-MGR-02:** *As a Supply Chain Director,* I want prioritized operational alerts highlighting depleted SKUs and delayed purchase orders so that I can intervene before customers experience fulfillment delays.
- **US-MGR-03:** *As an Operations Lead,* I want to approve or cancel submitted purchase orders so that rogue spending is prevented and inventory commitments are authorized.

---

## 2. Procurement & Inventory Specialist Stories
- **US-PROC-01:** *As a Procurement Specialist,* I want to register and monitor suppliers with their contractual lead times and historical on-time delivery rates so that I select the most reliable vendor for critical components.
- **US-PROC-02:** *As a Warehouse Intake Clerk,* I want to record partial or complete physical stock receipts against approved purchase orders so that physical warehouse quantities are updated immediately.
- **US-PROC-03:** *As an Inventory Controller,* I want to execute manual stock adjustments with mandatory reason documentation so that discrepancy resolutions are fully auditable.

---

## 3. Business & Functional Analyst Stories
- **US-BA-01:** *As a Business Analyst,* I want to access a structured requirements repository with categorized objectives and testable acceptance criteria so that I can demonstrate traceability throughout the software delivery lifecycle.
- **US-BA-02:** *As a Product Analyst,* I want to export filtered sales and inventory records to CSV with sanitized cells so that I can perform secondary modeling in Microsoft Excel or Power BI.
- **US-BA-03:** *As a Functional Analyst,* I want to run a rules engine that flags compressed gross margins and delayed vendor deliveries so that data-driven recommendations are surfaced to management.

---

## 4. System Administrator Stories
- **US-ADM-01:** *As a System Administrator,* I want to inspect immutable operational audit logs so that I can verify which user performed sensitive actions and when.
- **US-ADM-02:** *As a System Administrator,* I want to activate or deactivate product SKUs so that obsolete catalog items are removed from active sales intake.
